    How Scrap Radar works

    Your PC scans Craigslist, OfferUp, Trash Nothing, Facebook and Nextdoor every 15–30 minutes, removes ads and bait, estimates what each item is worth, and this app shows the best ones around you.

    Quick modes (top row)

    🔥 Just posted
    Free things posted in the last 6 hours, newest first. Best for grabbing stuff the moment it goes out.
    ⭐ Best near me
    Free and cheap items within your range, best payoff after gas first. The everyday view.
    Free only
    Only $0 listings, nearest first.
    Scrap metal
    Free scrap targets: metal, copper/aluminum, appliances (washers, dryers, dishwashers, AC units), filing cabinets, steel shelving, motorcycles, boat trailers and electronics (copiers, printers, stereos, computers).
    Big flips
    Things worth $40+ to resell.
    Yard sales
    Yard, garage and estate sales in the last 3 days.
    🧭 Route
    Type where you're headed (or tap the map): shows finds along the drive, and "Plan pickups along route" builds the stops in order.
    🚗 Road ahead
    While driving, only shows finds in the direction you're heading.

    Bar under the modes

    Filters
    Range, price (free / under $10 / $25 / $40), listing type, minimum value, posted within, source, sort, and keyword. "Reset filters" goes back to Best near me.
    Plan my run
    Picks the 8 best pickups in range and orders them into the shortest drive. Opens in Google Maps.

    On each listing

    Title
    Opens the original listing.
    FREE / $ · worth · net
    Price, estimated value (resale or scrap, whichever is higher), and what's left after the asking price and gas.
    + Run
    Adds it to your pickup run (tap again to remove).
    Message
    Copies a ready-to-send "Is this still available?" message and opens the listing so you can paste it.
    Go
    Turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps, to the street address when the listing has one.
    📍 line
    Green = real address or exact pin, you can drive straight there. Amber "Area only" = OfferUp/Facebook/Nextdoor and some Craigslist posts only give the neighborhood, so message the seller for the address first ("Go (area)" only gets you to the neighborhood). Plan my run now puts real-address stops first.
    More
    Value details, 📞 Check availability (Net Hero calls the seller for you, Craigslist only), Open listing, ✓ Got it (adds it to your Haul), Gone / skip (hides it), Ad / spam (hides it and every repeat of it).
    🕒 age
    How long ago it was posted: green under 3 hours, amber under a day. "(seen)" means the site doesn't show a post time, so it's when the radar first spotted it.
    Tags
    trailer? or 2 people = big or heavy · also on… = same item on another site · new = since you last looked. Faded cards are over 3 days old.
